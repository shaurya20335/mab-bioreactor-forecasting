# References and provenance

- Kumar et al. (2022), *Multi-objective optimization of monoclonal antibody
  production in bioreactor*, Chemical Engineering and Processing – Process
  Intensification, 180, 108720.
  [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cep.2021.108720).
  Source for the mechanistic equations and parameter table. See
  [model review](model_review.md) for transcription corrections and limitations.
- [Original B.Tech project brief](project_proposal.pdf), *Data Driven Modeling
  of monoclonal antibody (mAb) production in bioreactor using LSTM (Long
  Short-Term Memory) Neural Networks*. Preserved as supplied; its historical
  results are distinct from the completed benchmark. See
  [report alignment](report_alignment.md).
- [arXiv:2210.02200, version 1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2210.02200v1).
  Background reference supplied with the original project; it is not the
  source of the simulator's equations or the final experiment's results.

External paper downloads are represented by source links rather than duplicate
PDF copies. The final methodology and results are in [the final report](final_report.md).
